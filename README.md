The entry is a single deadpan sentence that kills the meeting it describes: "This meeting could have been an email, but then nobody would have ignored it together." It takes the classic complaint about pointless meetings and turns it into the punchline — the meeting's only real achievement was getting everyone to ignore the same email at the same time, in the same room. Anyone who has ever sat through an hour-long meeting that produced nothing will recognize it instantly, and after the line lands, there is no reason left to hold the meeting at all.
